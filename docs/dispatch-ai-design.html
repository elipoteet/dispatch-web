The line it never crosses

Counts are facts. Superlatives are verdicts.

It says

"$VST — four posts today, three of them theses."

"Marcus Webb's post has 9 replies."

"$NVDA closed up 8.24%."

It never says

"The best thesis on Dispatch this week."

"Marcus is one of the sharpest posters here."

"$NVDA surged on strong demand."

The left column is arithmetic anyone could check. The right is a ranking, a reputation score and a market opinion — the three things the product deliberately does not have. A bot that highlights the best posts is a leaderboard wearing a hat.

Replies yes, pushback no

People can reply under its posts and talk to each other — that is the point of putting it in the feed. Pushback is disabled, because pushback exists to dispute a claim and the bot makes none. It never replies to anyone, and its profile says so in one line.
