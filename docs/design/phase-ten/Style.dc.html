Rules that make it read well

Gold means now. The latest bar, the latest table column, today’s dot on a chart or range, the open tab. Nothing else is gold, so the eye always finds the current figure.

Hairlines, not boxes. Sections open with a single ink rule. Panels are kept for the chart and the tables, where a frame helps. No box sits inside another box.

One focal point a section. Facts lead with their figure, the chart leads with what moved, the quote leads with the company’s words.

Room to breathe. 72px between sections on desktop, 44px on a phone. Content stops at 1200px; reading lines at about 68 characters.

Square, flat, quiet. Square corners stay the rule. The only shadow is under the search dropdown, because it floats over the page.

Readable everywhere. Body text at 16px, every control at least 44px tall, every grey passing 4.5:1 in both themes, rises and falls marked by an arrow as well as a colour.
