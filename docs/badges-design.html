Verification badges

One rosette, four colours. The shape is identical across every tier so the badge always reads as "verified" at a glance; the colour is what tells you which kind of person you're reading. Twelve lobes, drawn as a single path, scaling cleanly from 13px in a feed row to a profile header.

The four tiers

Student

Verified by school email. The default, and the great majority of the site.

#3E76B8

Alum

Graduated. Same ladder, further along — the badge fades rather than changes shape.

#9A9384

Faculty

Professors and lecturers. Approved individually, never self-selected.

#0E1D33

Mentor

Working in the industry, invited by a school or club. The rarest badge on the site.

#B08430

All four hues come out of the existing palette — the blue is a lighter relative of the navy, the grey is warm so it sits with the cream, and gold is the brand accent held back for the rarest tier. No green and no red: on a site covered in price data those two already mean "up" and "down."

13px is the floor. Below that the twelve lobes turn to mush and the check loses its corners — if something needs a smaller mark, use a plain filled circle in the same colour rather than shrinking this one.

One component, one path, four modifier classes. Colours live as CSS custom properties (--v-student, --v-alum, --v-faculty, --v-mentor) that lighten in dark mode — navy in particular disappears on a dark ground, so faculty flips to a pale slate. Always give it a real aria-label: the badge carries meaning, so it is not decorative.
